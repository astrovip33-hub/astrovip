# AstroVip SEO Quality Policy

AstroVip prioritizes useful, original pages over raw page count.

## Publishing guardrails

- No automatic mass publication of thousands of indexable pages.
- A single commit may add at most 25 new indexable HTML pages.
- New indexable pages must have at least 250 visible words.
- Every new indexable page must have one H1, a self-referencing canonical URL, a descriptive title and meta description.
- Placeholder/test pages are blocked from production indexing.
- The legacy Atlas can remain accessible, but stays excluded from mass sitemap submission while bulk indexing is paused.
- Existing sitemap coverage validation remains mandatory.

These are internal AstroVip quality thresholds, not claimed Google ranking factors. They are intentionally conservative safeguards against thin or scaled low-value publishing.
